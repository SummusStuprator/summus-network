**Input:** repository or reproducible code, one agreed issue, and the acceptance condition.

**Deliverable:**
- reproduction or explicit reason the bug could not be reproduced;
- focused patch;
- regression test where practical;
- commands/results used to verify the change;
- concise limitation notes;
- one revision within the agreed scope.

**Acceptance:** the agreed focused test/reproduction passes and the patch is reviewable. Broader production-build claims are made only when actually verified.
